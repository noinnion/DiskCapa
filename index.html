FROM “DISK FULL” TO A CLEARER PICTURE

Three ways to find your space.

01 / SCAN

Start with the usual suspects.

Check known cache locations and developer tool leftovers. Choose a project folder to find its build output and dependency directories.

02 / DEEP SCAN

Find what you forgot about.

Look further for duplicates, large files, and build artifacts across your chosen location. Review the findings before removing anything.

03 / EXPLORE

See the bigger picture.

Follow an interactive sunburst chart into your folders. Understand where the space goes before deciding what to clean up.

YOUR FILES. YOUR CALL.

Understand first.
Clean up second.

Cleanup should be a decision you can explain. DiskCapa gives each finding a reason and a risk tier so you can review it with context.

  • ↶

    Trash by default

    Move items to the Trash, with a record of every removal. Dry run lets you preview a cleanup without deleting files.

  • ✓

    Review stays in your hands

    Items marked Review or Caution are never preselected. Scheduled scans report findings; they don’t delete files.

  • ⌁

    Optional AI second opinion

    Ask about an individual finding using a provider you configure. AI review is off by default and cannot authorize deletion. Requests send item metadata, not file contents.
